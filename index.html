1. How Quantitative Expected Goals (xG) & Bivariate Poisson Models Work

Modern football analytics evaluates club performance beyond raw final scorelines. By measuring the quality of every shot taken and conceded—factoring in shot distance, angle to goal, assist type, defensive pressure, and passage of play—Expected Goals (xG) quantifies the underlying attacking and defensive efficiency of each team over rolling 5-match, 10-match, and full-season windows.

PredictPro combines rolling xG rates with club ELO ratings and venue-specific home advantage coefficients to compute expected scoring rates (λhome and λaway). Using a 6×6 Bivariate Poisson probability matrix enhanced with the Dixon-Coles (ρ) low-scoring correlation adjustment, our engine calculates objective percentage probabilities for 1X2 match outcomes, Total Goals (Over/Under 2.5), Both Teams to Score (BTTS), and exact scoreline distributions.
